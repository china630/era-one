ERA BYO-EDR HubThird-party EDR telemetry ingestion

During migration or in a multi-vendor environment — ingest events from existing endpoint solutions into the unified ERA data lake and correlate in Workbench. No need to "rip and replace everything."

Purpose

Preserve investment in existing EDR and gradually consolidate the SOC around ERA as the unified "brain" in the perimeter.

Key capabilities

  • Ingestion adapters: API, syslog, standardized JSON.
  • Normalization to the platform's unified event format.
  • Indicator enrichment and display in Workbench.

Who it's for

Holdings, phased migration from foreign EDR, hybrid perimeters.

Licensing

Base adapter included in ERA Core; additional connectors available as options.
