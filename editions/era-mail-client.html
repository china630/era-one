ERA Mail ClientWebmail and clients

Webmail, desktop, and mobile — paired with ERA Mail Server server-side Outlook parity (Autodiscover, EWS, CalDAV, CardDAV, ActiveSync). Compatibility is on the server — no desktop plugins. Unified UX with ERA Chat and Conference.

Key capabilities

  • Webmail in the perimeter browser.
  • Outlook and mobile via Autodiscover — mass deployment without client plugins.
  • CalDAV/CardDAV for Apple Calendar, Thunderbird, and compatible clients.
  • ActiveSync for iOS/Android (gov pilot subset).
  • SSO via ERA One identity.

Included with ERA Mail Server. Hybrid transition: ERA Mail Connect (BFF to external IMAP/JMAP/EWS).
