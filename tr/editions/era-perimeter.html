ERA PerimeterNetwork security at the edge

Sovereign perimeter controls for the isolated contour: policy enforcement at the network edge, aligned with ERA Control telemetry and identity — without cloud phone-home.

Purpose

Close the gap between endpoint XDR and the network boundary. ERA Perimeter extends the one-platform model to edge policy so regulated environments keep inspection and decisions inside the customer perimeter.

  • Edge policy aligned with ERA One identity and admin.
  • Designed for on-prem / air-gap and Sovereign Hybrid profiles.
  • Works alongside ERA Control editions — modular licensing.

Key capabilities

  • Perimeter policy controls for regulated and air-gapped networks.
  • Integration path with ERA Control agents and the SOC portal.
  • Offline-friendly updates and Ed25519 licensing model shared with ERA One.

Who it's for

Banks, public sector and critical infrastructure that need network-edge controls under the same sovereign vendor as XDR and IT-Ops.

Licensing

Modular ERA Control family edition. Requires an ERA One platform deployment; exact SKU is fixed in the commercial proposal.
